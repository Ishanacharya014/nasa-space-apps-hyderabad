MISSION BRIEFING

What is the NASA Space Apps Challenge?

🌍

The Global Hackathon

NASA Space Apps is an international hackathon for coders, scientists, designers, storytellers, makers, builders, and technologists in cities around the world.

🛠️

What Happens Here?

Over 48 hours, teams utilize NASA's free and open data to address real-world problems on Earth and in space, creating innovative apps, games, and hardware.

🚀

Why Participate?

Connect with a global community, build your portfolio, learn new skills, and potentially have your project recognized by NASA and global space agencies.

🇮🇳

Why Hyderabad?

Hyderabad is India's rapidly growing hub for aerospace, technology, and innovation. Home to major IT parks and a vibrant startup ecosystem, it's the perfect launchpad for your Space Apps journey.

HOW TO PARTICIPATE

Who can join?

Absolutely anyone! Whether you're a beginner, student, professional, or hobbyist. We need all skills: developers, designers, artists, and storytellers.
